Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 17, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Bitcoin Halving Countdown, a free, open-source web utility designed to track Bitcoin halvings in real time ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains our data practices. We prioritize user privacy and operate on a no-collection principle. The utility runs entirely in your browser, fetching public blockchain data when needed, but we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

As a client-side application, we avoid gathering any user data:

  • Personal Information: No names, emails, IP addresses, or device details.
  • Usage Analytics: No tracking of visits, page views, or behavior—no cookies, pixels, or third-party tools like Google Analytics.
  • Input Data: Any interactions (e.g., halving calculator inputs) are processed locally and never sent to servers.
  • Blockchain Queries: Live data (block height, progress) is fetched from public APIs like mempool.space directly by your browser—we receive nothing.

Your experience remains completely anonymous.

3. How Data Is Handled

The utility functions purely client-side:

  • Real-Time Features: Countdown, block height, and progress use JavaScript to query public endpoints in your browser.
  • Calculations: Halving estimates and schedules are computed locally using Bitcoin's fixed rules.
  • Static Content: Historical tables (price impacts, difficulty) are hardcoded for reliability—no external loads.

No data leaves your device unless your browser requests public blockchain info, which is anonymous and standard for any Bitcoin explorer.

4. Third-Party Services

We use minimal external resources:

  • Public APIs (e.g., mempool.space) for live block data—your browser contacts them directly.
  • Bootstrap CDN for styling—no tracking.

No advertising, analytics, or social integrations that could collect data.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies, local storage for tracking, or any persistent identifiers. Sessions are stateless.

6. Security

Since no data is collected, there is nothing to breach. We recommend using updated browsers and secure connections (HTTPS) for general web safety.

7. Children's Privacy

The utility is general audience; we do not target children under 13 and collect no data regardless.

8. International Compliance

By collecting nothing, we comply with GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws by design.

9. Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted here with new effective date.

10. Contact

Questions? Open an issue on our GitHub repository.

We believe privacy is fundamental—enjoy the utility with complete peace of mind.
